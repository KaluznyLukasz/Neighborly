Support

Email contact@example.com. We reply within two business days, and review safety reports within 24 hours.

Report a problem

  • A post or alert: open it and tap Report Offer or Report Alert.
  • A message or review: touch and hold it, then tap Report.
  • A person: open their profile and tap Report User.

Reports are anonymous. If someone is in danger, call 112 first.

Block someone

Open their profile and tap Block User. You won’t see their posts or alerts, and they can’t message you or respond to your posts. To undo it, go to Profile → Settings → Blocked Users.

Change your password

Go to Profile → Settings → Change Password, or tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. We’ll email you a reset link.

Delete your account

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account and enter your password. This permanently deletes your profile, posts, alerts, messages and the reviews you wrote, and cancels open exchanges. If you can’t sign in, email us from your account’s email address and we’ll delete it for you.

Get a copy of your data

Email us from your account’s email address and we’ll send you a copy within one month.

Safety tips

  • Check the other person’s rating and reviews before you agree.
  • Keep conversations in the app until you’ve agreed on the details.
  • Meet in a public, well-lit place for a first exchange, or bring someone with you.
  • Never send money in advance to someone you haven’t met. Neighborly doesn’t process payments.
  • Trust your instincts and cancel if something feels wrong.
