Neighborly

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Neighborly helps neighbors lend, borrow and help each other, and share short-lived alerts with people nearby. This policy explains what personal data Neighborly collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Neighborly iOS app and its widgets.

Who is responsible

Neighborly is operated by Łukasz Kałużny, Poland (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact us at contact@example.com.

What we collect

Account

  • Your name, email address and password. Firebase Authentication stores the password in hashed form and we never see it.
  • A user ID, the date you joined, and when you accepted the Terms of Use.

Profile

  • An optional profile photo and bio.
  • Your rating and the reviews other members leave about you.

What you post and send

  • Posts: title, description, category, optional photo, the address you enter, and the map location of that address.
  • Alerts: title, details, optional photo, and your device’s location at the moment you post.
  • Exchanges: requests to help, their status, the message you add, and any agreed or return date.
  • Messages you send in exchanges and alert conversations.
  • Reviews you write, including the rating and comment.
  • Your saved posts, the people you block, and reports you submit.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device’s location to show posts and alerts near you. We don’t store your location history. Your location is saved only when you post an alert, as the alert’s location. You can turn location access off in iOS Settings at any time.

Data that stays on your device

Notification preferences, appearance settings, scheduled reminders and widget data are stored only on your device and aren’t sent to us.

Technical data

Our service providers process technical data such as your IP address and device information when the app connects, as needed to deliver the service and keep it secure. Neighborly has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

What other people can see

  • Signed-in members can see your name, profile photo, bio, join date, rating and reviews, your posts (including their address and map location) and your alerts (including their location).
  • Messages are visible only to the people in that conversation.
  • Your email address is never shown to other members.

Why we use your data

  • To provide Neighborly: your account, posts, alerts, messages, exchanges, reviews and reminders. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the community safe: filtering offensive language, handling reports, removing content and closing accounts that break the rules, and preventing abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a safe service (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Location and notifications: only after you allow them in iOS. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw in iOS Settings.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as responding to lawful requests from authorities (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC) provides authentication, the database and the hosting of this website. Google processes data on our behalf under its data processing terms. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • Apple provides maps, address search and address lookup. The addresses you type for a post are sent to Apple to find their location, under Apple’s privacy policy.

We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep data

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • Alerts disappear from the app 48 hours after posting. They stay stored with your account until you or your account delete them.
  • When you delete your account in Settings → Delete Account, we immediately delete your profile, posts, alerts, messages, exchanges, the reviews you wrote, and your saved and blocked lists. Open exchanges with other members are cancelled.
  • We keep reports for up to 12 months to handle repeat abuse and legal claims. Reviews other members wrote about you are no longer shown once your profile is deleted. We will remove them on request.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or port your data, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time. You can edit your profile and delete your account in the app. For anything else, email contact@example.com. We’ll reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl.

Age requirement

Neighborly is for people aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger, and we delete accounts we learn belong to a minor.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and protected by access rules that limit who can read each record. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we’ll tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
